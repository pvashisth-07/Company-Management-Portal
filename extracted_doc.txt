Project Objective
To develop a centralized portal for managing institutional interactions with recruiting companies and industry organizations, including company profiles, contacts, visits, recruitment activities, and engagement history.
Expected Outcome
A centralized and organized system for recording, managing, and tracking company profiles, contacts, visits, recruitment activities, and
institutional engagement history.
Project Background Current Problem
Managing company profiles, contact details, visits, recruitment activities, and engagement history through scattered or manual records can be difficult. It can also make tracking previous company interactions less efficient.
Proposed Solution
The Company Engagement Portal will centralize company-related information and institutional interactions in a single system. It will help authorized users efficiently manage company profiles, contacts, visits, recruitment activities, and engagement history.
Project Vision
The Company Engagement Portal aims to provide a centralized and organized platform for managing institutional interactions with companies and industry organizations. It will simplify the management of company information, recruitment activities, visits, contacts, and engagement history.
Problem Statement
Institutions need to manage interactions with recruiting companies and industry organizations, including company profiles, contacts, visits, recruitment activities, and engagement history. Managing this information through scattered or manual records can make it difficult to track interactions and maintain up-to-date information. Therefore, a centralized Company Engagement Portal is needed to organize and efficiently manage these activities.
Project Objectives
The initial objectives should be:
To maintain centralized profiles of recruiting companies and industry organizations.
To manage and update company contact details.
To record and track company visits to the institution.
To manage recruitment-related activities and their details.
To maintain the history of institutional engagement with companies.
To provide easy access to company-related information for authorized users.
To improve the organization and tracking of company interactions.
To reduce dependency on scattered or manual records.

User Classes
Training & Placement (T&P) Coordinator
The T&P Coordinator is the primary operator of the system, responsible for maintaining company-related information.
They shall be able to:
authenticate;
create and manage company profiles;
add and update company contacts;
record and view company visits;
create, edit and submit recruitment activities for approval;
view engagement history;
access reports.
Company Representative
Company representatives are the external point of contact for a company and provide information used by the institution.
They shall be able to:
share company and contact information with the T&P Coordinator (offline/through the Coordinator, not direct system login in the initial version);
participate in recorded visits and recruitment activities.
Approving Authority (Faculty/Institute Authority)
The approving authority reviews recruitment activities submitted by coordinators. The approver shall be able to:
review submitted recruitment activities;
approve recruitment activities;
reject recruitment activities;
provide comments/reasons;
view approval history.
Administrator
The administrator shall:
manage users;
assign roles;
manage system-level configuration;
monitor system activity;
access administrative reports.
Institute Management
Management users shall have access to appropriate aggregated reports and dashboards for monitoring company engagement and recruitment activity across the institution.
System Context
Functional Requirements
Authentication and Account Management
FR-001 — User Login: The system shall allow a registered user to authenticate using valid credentials.
FR-002 — Authentication Failure: The system shall notify the user when authentication fails without unnecessarily revealing sensitive authentication information.
FR-003 — Logout: The system shall allow authenticated users to terminate their session.
FR-004 — Role Assignment: The system shall allow authorized administrators to assign roles to users.
FR-005 — Role-Based Access: The system shall restrict system functions according to the user's assigned role.
Company Profile Management
FR-006 — Create Company Profile: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to create a company profile.
FR-007 — Company Profile Information: The system shall allow a coordinator to specify: company name; industry/sector; address; website; primary contact; status; notes.
FR-008 — Edit Company Profile: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to modify an existing company profile.
FR-009 — Deactivate Company Profile: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to deactivate/archive a company profile.
FR-010 — View Company Profile: The system shall allow authorized users to view complete company profile details.
Company Contact Management
FR-011 — Add Company Contact: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to add a contact person for a company.
FR-012 — Contact Information: The system shall allow a coordinator to specify: name; designation; email; phone; department.
FR-013 — Edit Company Contact: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to update contact details.
FR-014 — Remove Company Contact: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to remove an outdated contact.
FR-015 — View Company Contacts: The system shall allow authorized users to view all contacts associated with a company.
Company Visit Management
FR-016 — Record Company Visit: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to record a company visit.
FR-017 — Visit Information: The system shall allow a coordinator to specify: visit date; purpose; attendees; outcome; notes.
FR-018 — Edit Visit Record: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to modify a visit record.
FR-019 — View Visit History: The system shall allow authorized users to view the visit history of a company.
Recruitment Activity Management
FR-020 — Create Recruitment Activity: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to create a recruitment activity.
FR-021 — Recruitment Activity Information: The system shall allow a coordinator to specify: title; company; type; date; eligibility criteria; positions offered; status.
FR-022 — Save Draft: The system shall allow an incomplete recruitment activity to be saved as a draft.
FR-023 — Edit Recruitment Activity: The system shall allow an authorized coordinator to modify a recruitment activity subject to its current status.
FR-024 — Submit for Approval: The system shall allow a coordinator to submit a recruitment activity for approval.
FR-025 — Approve Recruitment Activity: The system shall allow an authorized approver to approve a recruitment activity.
FR-026 — Reject Recruitment Activity: The system shall allow an authorized approver to reject a recruitment activity.
FR-027 — Rejection Reason: The system shall allow an approver to provide a reason when rejecting a recruitment activity.
FR-028 — Publish Recruitment Activity: The system shall mark an approved recruitment activity as published according to defined rules.
FR-029 — Cancel Recruitment Activity: The system shall allow an authorized user to cancel a recruitment activity.
Engagement History
FR-030 — Log Engagement Record: The system shall automatically log an engagement record whenever a visit, contact update, or recruitment activity occurs.
FR-031 — View Engagement History: The system shall allow authorized users to view the complete engagement history of a company.
FR-032 — Engagement Timeline: The system shall present engagement history in chronological order.
Search and Access
FR-033 — Company Search: The system shall allow authorized users to search for companies.
FR-034 — Company Filtering: The system shall allow authorized users to filter companies by relevant attributes (industry, status, etc.).
FR-035 — View Company Details: The system shall display complete relevant information for a selected company.
Reporting
FR-036 — Engagement Report: The system shall generate company engagement reports for authorized users.
FR-037 — Recruitment Activity Report: The system shall generate recruitment activity summaries.
FR-038 — Visit Report: The system shall generate company visit reports.
FR-039 — Administrative Dashboard: The system shall provide appropriate summary information to authorized administrators.

UC-006 — Record Company Visit Primary Actor: T&P Coordinator Preconditions:
Coordinator is authenticated.
Company profile exists. Main Flow:

UC-008 — Submit Recruitment Activity
Actor(s) involved
T&P Coordinator.
Starting condition
The coordinator is authenticated and a recruitment activity exists in a status that permits submission (normally a draft).
Main sequence
Coordinator selects the recruitment activity draft.
System displays the activity details.
Coordinator reviews the recruitment activity details.
Coordinator selects “Submit for Approval.”
System validates the required information and current status.
System marks the activity as Submitted.
The submitted activity becomes available for the approval process.
Alternative / exception
If required information is incomplete or the activity status does not permit submission, the system displays a validation error and the coordinator corrects/edits the activity before retrying.
Expected outcome
The recruitment activity is in Submitted status and is ready for review by the Approving Authority.

The role classes TPCoordinator and ApprovingAuthority specialize User because the requirements distinguish their responsibilities and permissions.
Company, Contact, Visit, RecruitmentActivity, Approval, EngagementHistory and EngagementRecord are domain classes grounded directly in the Assignment 2 data requirements and business rules.

The classes are deliberately limited to responsibilities required by the selected use cases and their stated preconditions, main flows, alternatives and postconditions.

4.3 Component specification — UC-006: VisitService.recordVisit()
Item
Details
Component

Visit (state RECORDED) or error

FUNCTION recordVisit(data, userId)  IF NOT validationService.validateCompanyExists(data.companyId) THEN      THROW NotFoundException("Company profile does not exist. Please create the profile first.")  result = validationService.validateRequiredFields(data)   // date, purpose, attendees, outcome  IF result.isValid = false THEN      visit.state = ERROR      THROW ValidationException(result.errors)               // shown on S3  visit = NEW Visit(data)                                    // state = ENTERED  visit.state = VALIDATING  IF visit.validate() = false THEN THROW ValidationException  BEGIN TRANSACTION      visit.state = RECORDED      visitRepo.save(visit)      engagementHistoryService.appendRecord(data.companyId, VISIT, data.purpose, userId)      visit.state = LOGGED  COMMIT  ON ERROR in transaction: ROLLBACK, log error, THROW "Visit could not be saved. Please try again."  RETURN visit
Decisions and exceptions: company missing → NotFoundException; mandatory field missing → ValidationException; database failure → rollback so a visit is never saved without its engagement record (NFR-012).

FUNCTION submitActivity(activityId, userId)  activity = activityRepo.findById(activityId)  IF activity = NULL THEN THROW NotFoundException  IF activity.createdBy != userId THEN THROW UnauthorizedException  r1 = validationService.validateRequiredFields(activity)   // title, company, type, date,                                                            // eligibility, positions  r2 = validationService.validateStatus(activity)           // must be DRAFT or REVISION  IF r1.isValid = false OR r2.isValid = false THEN      THROW ValidationException(r1.errors + r2.errors)      // shown on S4  IF activity.status = CANCELLED THEN THROW InvalidStateException   // BR-007  activity.submit()                                         // sets status = SUBMITTED  activityRepo.save(activity)  RETURN activity
Decisions and exceptions: missing field → "Cannot submit: <field> is required."; wrong status → "Only DRAFT or REVISION activities can be submitted."; cancelled activity cannot be edited or submitted (BR-007).
4.5 Component specification — UC-009: ApprovalService.approve()
Item
Details
Component
Approval Management
Input

Output

FUNCTION approve(activityId, approverId, comments)  IF NOT user(approverId).hasRole(APPROVER) THEN THROW UnauthorizedException  activity = activityRepo.findById(activityId)  IF activity = NULL THEN THROW NotFoundException  IF activity.status NOT IN (SUBMITTED, UNDER_REVIEW) THEN      THROW InvalidStateException("This activity is not available for review.")  IF activity.status = SUBMITTED THEN activity.changeStatus(UNDER_REVIEW)  BEGIN TRANSACTION      approval = NEW Approval(activityId, approverId, APPROVED, comments, NOW())      approvalRepo.save(approval)                       // approver + decision date stored      activity.changeStatus(APPROVED)      activityRepo.save(activity)      engagementHistoryService.appendRecord(activity.companyId, ACTIVITY, "Activity approved", approverId)  COMMIT  RETURN approvalFUNCTION reject(activityId, approverId, reason)          // alternative flow UC-010  IF reason is empty THEN THROW ValidationException("Please provide a reason for rejection.")  ... same steps with decision = REJECTED and status = REJECTED
Decisions and exceptions: not an approver → UnauthorizedException; activity in another state → InvalidStateException; database failure → rollback so status and approval record stay consistent.
